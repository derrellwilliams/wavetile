Download Wavetile

Wavetile is in public beta on TestFlight, Apple's app for testing new apps. From install to your first offline album in a few minutes.

Download the beta

01

What you need

An iPhone on iOS 26.1 or later, and a Jellyfin server with a music library. It can be your own server or one someone shares with you.

02

Join the beta

Install TestFlight from the App Store. Then open the Wavetile beta link on your iPhone, tap Accept, then Install. Wavetile appears on your Home Screen like any other app.

New builds show up in TestFlight; turn on automatic updates there to get them as they land. Each build works for 90 days, and a newer one is always out before then.

03

Sign in

Enter your server's address, such as https://music.example.com, with your username and password. Or use Quick Connect: start it in Wavetile and approve the code from Jellyfin on a device where you're already signed in.

Servers reachable from the internet need https. Plain http works only on your home network, so your password and token never cross the internet unencrypted.

04

Let the library sync

The first sync copies your library's details (not the music files) to your iPhone. After that, Home and search are instant and work offline. Pull down on Home to refresh.

05

Download for offline

Tap the download button on an album or playlist. Pick the quality in Settings → Downloads: MP3 256 kbps to save space, or Original for the file as it is on your server. Downloads continue in the background, with progress on the Lock Screen.

06

Turn on podcasts

Open Settings and turn on Show Podcasts. Search for shows or import your OPML file. More about podcasts

07

Add widgets

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose Wavetile for Now Playing or Recently Played.

Send feedback

Take a screenshot in Wavetile and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open Wavetile in TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback. Crashes are reported to the developer through TestFlight if you allow it. Stuck? See Support.
