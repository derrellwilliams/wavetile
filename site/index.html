Questions

What is Wavetile?

Wavetile is a Jellyfin music player for iPhone with a full podcast player built in. It streams and downloads the music on your Jellyfin server, and plays your podcasts in the same app.

How is Wavetile different from other Jellyfin apps?

Wavetile adds podcasts to your Jellyfin music, with directory search, OPML import, chapters, transcripts and subscriptions synced through your server. It also keeps a local copy of your library, so browsing is instant and works offline, even with 50,000 tracks.

Does Wavetile work offline?

Yes. You can browse your library with no connection and download albums, playlists and podcast episodes, as 256 kbps MP3 or the original file such as FLAC.

Does Wavetile collect my data?

No. There's no account, no analytics and no tracking. Wavetile talks to your Jellyfin server, and to podcast feeds and Apple's podcast directory if you use podcasts. Privacy policy

What do I need to use Wavetile?

An iPhone on iOS 26.1 or later and a Jellyfin server with music. Sign in with your server address or Quick Connect.
