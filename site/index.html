Questions

What is Wavetile?

Wavetile is a native Jellyfin music and podcast player for iPhone. It streams and downloads the music on your own Jellyfin server, with gapless playback, Instant Mix radio, widgets and an optional built-in podcast player.

Is Wavetile a good Jellyfin music app for iPhone?

Wavetile is built only for Jellyfin music on iPhone. It mirrors your library on the device so browsing is instant and works offline, plays albums gaplessly, and uses native iOS 26 design, widgets and Live Activities.

Does Wavetile work offline?

Yes. Wavetile keeps a local copy of your library so you can browse without a connection, and you can download albums, playlists and podcast episodes to play anywhere, as 256 kbps MP3 or the original file.

Does Wavetile support podcasts?

Yes, as an optional layer you turn on in Settings. Search the podcast directory, follow by URL or import OPML, and get chapters, transcripts and downloads. Subscriptions sync through your Jellyfin server.

Does Wavetile collect my data?

No. Wavetile has no account, no analytics and no tracking. It talks to your Jellyfin server, and to podcast feeds and Apple's podcast directory only if you use podcasts. Your sign-in token is stored in the iOS Keychain.

Does Wavetile support CarPlay?

CarPlay support is built and waiting on Apple's CarPlay audio approval. It will arrive in an update once Apple grants it.

What do I need to use Wavetile?

An iPhone on iOS 26.1 or later and a Jellyfin server with music. Sign in with your server address or Quick Connect.
