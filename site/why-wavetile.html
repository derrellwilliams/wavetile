Why Wavetile

A Jellyfin music app for iPhone, with podcasts built in.

If your music lives on a Jellyfin server, Wavetile gives you one app for your library and your podcasts, designed for iPhone.

Podcasts next to your music

Podcasts get their own tab, and Up Next in Podcasts sits on Home beside Recently Played. Search the directory, import OPML from your old app, and get chapters, transcripts and downloads. Subscriptions and progress sync through your Jellyfin account. How podcasts work

Instant, even with huge libraries

Wavetile keeps a local copy of your library on your iPhone, so Home, search and sorting don't wait on the server. It's tested with 50,000 tracks, and you can browse with no connection at all.

Made for listening

  • Gapless playback for live albums and mixes
  • Start Radio: Jellyfin Instant Mix from any song, album or artist
  • Downloads as 256 kbps MP3 or the original file, such as FLAC
  • Sleep timer, AirPlay, Lock Screen and Control Center controls

Native to iPhone

Built in SwiftUI for iOS 26: Now Playing and Recently Played widgets, a Live Activity for downloads, and Siri. CarPlay is built and waiting on Apple's approval.

Private by default

No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your sign-in token stays in the iOS Keychain, and plain http is only allowed for servers on your home network. Privacy policy

Other Jellyfin music apps

Jellyfin has several good iPhone clients, including Finamp, Jellify and Manet. Choose Wavetile if you want your podcasts in the same app as your music, a library that's instant and works offline, and an app that collects nothing.
