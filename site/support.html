Signing in

Enter your Jellyfin server's address (for example https://music.example.com), or use Quick Connect: start it in Wavetile, then approve the code from Jellyfin on another device.

Use an https:// address for servers reachable from the internet. Plain http only works for servers on your home network, which keeps your password and token off unencrypted connections.

Downloads and offline

Tap the download button on an album or playlist. Choose MP3 256 kbps (smaller) or Original (the file as stored on your server) under Settings → Downloads. Downloaded music lives in the Downloaded tab and plays with no connection.

Podcasts

Podcasts are off by default. Turn on Show Podcasts in Settings, then search the directory, paste a feed URL or import an OPML file from another app.

Something's not right

  • Library looks out of date: pull to refresh on Home. A full sync runs after you sign in.
  • Can't connect: check the address opens in Safari on the same network, and that your Jellyfin server is up to date.
  • Playback stops on cellular: try MP3 downloads, or check that your server allows transcoding.

Requirements

iPhone on iOS 26.1 or later, and a Jellyfin server with a music library.
