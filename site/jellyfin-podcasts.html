Why Podcasts Download

Podcasts with Jellyfin, on iPhone

Wavetile adds a full podcast player to your Jellyfin setup.

Your music comes from your Jellyfin server and your podcasts come from their own feeds, but you listen to both in one app. Your subscriptions and progress follow your Jellyfin account to any iPhone you sign in on.

01

Find shows

  • Search the podcast directory or browse top shows
  • Paste any RSS feed URL
  • Import an OPML file from your current podcast app, or export one to leave
02

Listen

  • Continue Listening, Latest Episodes, and Up Next on Home
  • Chapters, and Podcasting 2.0 transcripts you can tap to jump to that moment
  • Playback speed, sleep timer and episode downloads for offline
03

Synced through Jellyfin

Subscriptions, playback positions and played episodes are saved to your Jellyfin user's preferences, so there's no extra account or sync service. Sign in on a new iPhone and your shows are there.

Only feed URLs, episode IDs and timestamps are stored. Private feeds (with a token or password in the URL) stay on your device. Anyone who can sign in as your Jellyfin user, including server admins, can read these preferences.

04

Turn it on

Podcasts are off by default. In Wavetile, open Settings and turn on Show Podcasts. Download and set up
